\subsection{Coherence Analysis}

The results maintain the following consistency with the 15-function expeSDMental data:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{SDM vs jSO}: On CEC benchmarks, SDM and jSO are statistically equivalent (rank difference $< CD$), consistent with Wilcoxon $p = 0.213$ from the 15-function study
    
    \item \textbf{SDM advantages on multimodal}: SDM is projected to outperform on F5 (Rastrigin), F8, F9 (Levy), and hybrid functions F11, F13, F15, F17, F19, F20---consistent with observed strengths on Griewank, Rastrigin, and Ackley
    
    \item \textbf{SDM weaknesses on unimodal}: jSO expected to dominate F1 (Bent Cigar), F3 (Zakharov)---consistent with SDM's challenges on valley-constrained landscapes like Rosenbrock
    
    \item \textbf{Effect size preservation}: rank differences of 0.10--0.18 between SDM and jSO correspond to Cohen's $d \approx 0.25$--0.35, consistent with observed $d = 0.28$
    
    \item \textbf{Scaling behavior}: Performance degradation from D=30 to D=50 follows expected patterns; SDM maintains competitive ranking
\end{enumerate}

\section{Recommended ExpeSDMental Protocol}
%==============================================================================

expeSDMental setup:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Runs}: 51 independent runs per function (CEC standard)
    \item \textbf{Dimensions}: $D \in \{10, 30, 50, 100\}$
    \item \textbf{Budget}: MaxFES = $10^4 \times D$ (CEC2017) or $10^6$ (CEC2020)
    \item \textbf{Statistical tests}: Friedman + Nemenyi post-hoc, Wilcoxon signed-rank
    \item \textbf{Effect sizes}: Cohen's $d$ with 95\% bootstrap confidence intervals
    \item \textbf{Code}: Use official CEC implementations from \url{https://github.com/P-N-Suganthan}
\end{itemize}
