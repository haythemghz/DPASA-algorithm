CEC2017 SECTION (PSDMARY) - WHAT TO INCLUDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. AGGREGATED CATEGORY TABLE (not 30 individual rows)

   - Benefit: 4 rows instead of 30, much more readable

2. STATISTICAL SUMMARY TABLE
   - Friedman ranks with p-value
   - Win/Tie/Loss counts vs each baseline
   - Effect sizes (Cohen's d) for key comparisons

3. CRITICAL DIFFERENCE DIAGRAM
   - Single figure showing Nemenyi post-hoc results
   - Visual proof of statistical significance
   - Replaces verbose pairwise comparison tables

4. CONVERGENCE PLOTS (SELECT 4 REPRESENTATIVE FUNCTIONS)
   - One unimodal (F1 or F3)
