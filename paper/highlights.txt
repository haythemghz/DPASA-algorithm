Research Highlights

• DPASA introduces a dual-population architecture that decouples search strategy evolution from solution finding

• A negation operator provides deterministic adversarial exploration in strategy parameter space, fundamentally different from opposition-based learning

• DPASA achieves Friedman rank 2.00 on CEC 2017, ranking second after L-SHADE (1.97) and outperforming jSO (2.03)

• Demonstrated superior robustness on deceptive multimodal landscapes while honestly acknowledging limitations on unimodal functions

• Validated on six engineering problems with near-optimal solutions (gaps ≤0.11% on five problems) and identified structural limitations on the Pressure Vessel problem
